Descriptive Statistics
the process of collecting organizing and describing data so that we can understand what the data looks limme

3 main things:
1. where is the data centered- mean, median, mode
2. how spread out the data is- range varience, standatrd deviation, IQR (Q3-Q1)
3. how does the distribution looks like- min and max, % and quartiles, skewness, outliers, histograms and box plots

real world examples- 
1. employess salaries- in case of outlier go with median 
2. customer spending
3. Student marks
